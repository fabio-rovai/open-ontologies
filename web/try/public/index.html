What this is, and is not

    
An induced ontology is a hypothesis about the sheet, not a truth about the domain. Three choices are deliberate: cardinality becomes a shape and never owl:FunctionalProperty, because under OWL RL a functional property with two values does not reject the second row, it identifies the two objects (prp-fp); observed numeric ranges are reported and never made sh:minInclusive; numbered columns (topping1…topping7) become one multi-valued property, and the group is reported so a false merge is visible. Strike what your domain does not support; the evidence column says what each line rests on.

    
Same thing from an MCP client, on your own files: onto_induce {"path": "sheet.csv"}. From the shell: open-ontologies batch with induce sheet.csv --out ./induced.
